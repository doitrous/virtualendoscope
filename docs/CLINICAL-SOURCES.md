## Audit decisions

- Preserve all 16 lessons, seven cases, existing ids and sample-count contracts. A duplicate draft of the world content was removed; the recovered training content is authoritative.
- Fasting is intake-specific and risk-based. Ordinary healthy-elective intervals do not guarantee an empty stomach. Case personas give actual illustrative intake times, not an all-solids six-hour rule.
- GLP-1 preparation follows the 2024 multisociety approach: most patients can continue, while higher-risk circumstances need a clinical-team plan. Do not teach blanket stopping of antithrombotics or routine laboratory panels.
- Consent includes capacity, communication needs, individualized risks, alternatives and the opportunity to decline. Link to the 2023 BSG update rather than only a technical curriculum.
- Standard precautions and appropriate PPE apply to diagnostic endoscopy. Surgical scrub is not a universal requirement. Reprocessing now explicitly includes precleaning, device-specific leak testing, manual channel cleaning, inspection and drying/storage; unresolved defects prevent use.
- The selected photo profile is ESGE 2025. Its lesser/greater curvature viewing sequence differs from the examples in BSG 2026; these are named profiles rather than a claim that one exact sequence is universal. Safety and tolerance override an inspection timer.
- Uncomplicated LA A/B reflux esophagitis needs no routine confirmation biopsy; LA C/D or atypical areas merit targeted sampling. Typical uncomplicated duodenal ulcers need no routine biopsy. Suspected varices are not forceps-sampling targets in the simulator.
- The suspected-Barrett case retains the **ACG 2022 initial diagnostic profile of at least eight samples** for its illustrative qualifying segment. This must not be confused with a fixed count for all surveillance cases; the Seattle protocol uses targeted abnormalities plus four-quadrant sampling at defined intervals.
- The gastric-ulcer case retains six feasible edge/base samples. An unbiopsied ulcer needs repeat assessment within **2 weeks** under the 2025 consensus; a sampled ulcer has a treatment, histology and healing-review plan (the 2017 BSG quality standard uses **6–8 weeks**).
- The celiac case is already selected for histology: **four D2 plus two bulb samples**, separately identified, under the BSG 2025/2026 profile. A specialist no-biopsy pathway has additional eligibility considerations and is not automatically triggered by appearance or a single serology threshold in this simulator.
- Recovery is assessed against baseline, not a fixed timer. Give safe-intake advice after throat anesthetic, written contacts/warning symptoms, appropriate IV-sedation escort/restrictions and named pathology ownership.

## Confidence and remaining review

Confidence is high for the directly checked preparation, monitoring, sampling and follow-up principles. Local medication decisions, staffing, rescue procedures, transmission precautions and discharge instructions remain patient- and setting-specific. No cited source validates synthetic lesion measurements, diagnostic accuracy or clinical competence. The 2026 celiac guideline is linked for specialist eligibility; this audit does not implement a general no-biopsy decision algorithm.

The contract check verifies unique source references, review dates, publication years, valid lesson answer indices and preservation of the seven cases and indicated sampling counts. It does not replace clinician review. A clinician should review educational deployment and revisit content when linked guidance changes.
